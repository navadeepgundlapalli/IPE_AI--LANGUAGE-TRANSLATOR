Questions, answered

Quick answers before you start.

Is Lingoverse free?

You can try it right here with no account. Translations use your Claude session.

How accurate is it?

It is AI-generated and usually strong, which is why the reverse check exists. For legal or medical text, always have a professional verify it.

Where is my history stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing site data removes it.

Can I choose how formal it sounds?

Yes. Pick one of six tones before you press Translate.

Why is the Listen voice missing for some languages?

Voices come from your device. If it lacks one for a language, install it in your system settings.
